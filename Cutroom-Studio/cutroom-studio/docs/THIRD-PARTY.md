# Third-party assets, dependencies, and notices

Cutroom artwork and product branding are independent. No OpusClip branding, source, bundles, or media are included. The stylized podcast photo in the dashboard is original generated artwork; it is not clip output.

## Real sample footage

The three editable examples are resized encodes of the indicated NASA media, with original audio. JSON and thumbnails were generated by `scripts/prepare_samples.py` from actual speech and images. They are not fabricated highlights. Credits also appear behind each sample's info button.

- **The next trip to the Moon:** [NASA SVS, Christina Koch Artemis II crew profile](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14934/), NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center. Source `ArtemisIICrew_Profiles_Christina_Koch_16x9.webm`.
- **How galaxies change:** [NASA SVS, The Evolution of Galaxies](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/12676/), NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center. Source `Evolution_of_Galaxies_H264.mp4`.
- **Finding my way to space:** [NASA Astronaut Biographical Videos, Jeanette Epps](https://archive.org/details/NASAAstronautBiographicalVideos), NASA / Johnson Space Center. Source `2015-00409_Epps_FINAL_01.mp4` (Public Domain Mark in the archive).

See [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). NASA identifiers incidentally visible in source footage are not used as Cutroom branding. No NASA endorsement is implied. Review source-specific third-party notices before reusing or launching a commercial derivative campaign.

## Tooling, fonts, and models

- REA is development tooling only. [REA MIT license](licenses/REA-MIT.txt); [upstream](https://github.com/morluto/rea).
- Interface fonts [DM Sans](licenses/DM-Sans-OFL.txt) and [Manrope](licenses/Manrope-OFL.txt) are SIL Open Font License 1.1. Other rendering fonts come from the deployed OS; custom assets are supplied by users.
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) (MIT), [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) (MIT) and the [Systran base conversion](https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-base). [Xenova MiniLM ONNX](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) derives from [all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) (Apache-2.0); English-language semantic rank signal. [Sherpa-ONNX](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) and [3D-Speaker CAM++](https://github.com/modelscope/3D-Speaker), used for approximate speaker clustering; review the selected checkpoint and training-data policies for commercial deployment. [OpenCV Zoo YuNet](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet) has a model-specific license; see its upstream directory. Downloaded model weights are not included in the source bundle.
- FFmpeg is an external installed binary. Debian’s build includes libass and libx264; check its configuration and corresponding LGPL/GPL obligations if distributing an image or binary, and review the relevant codec licensing terms for your use. Python dependency versions are in `requirements.txt`.
